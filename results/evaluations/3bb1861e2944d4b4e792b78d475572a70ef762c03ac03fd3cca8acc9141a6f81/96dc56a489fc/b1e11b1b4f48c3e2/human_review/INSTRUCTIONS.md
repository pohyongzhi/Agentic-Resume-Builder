# Independent human review

Reviewers must work independently and must not inspect `review_mapping.private.json`.
Read all three documents in each packet item. Rate every rubric dimension from 1 to 5
using the same anchors as the automated judge. For each extracted claim, use exactly one
claim-support label: `supported`, `contradicted`, or `unsupported`. Do not infer facts not
present in the original resume. The forms are intentionally blank; automated findings are
not human-confirmed until completed ratings are imported and agreement is calculated.
